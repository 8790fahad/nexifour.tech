Our approach

Start simple, go deeper only when it is justified

We do not assume that training a model from scratch is the right answer. We choose the lightest approach that solves the problem.

1

Open-weight models

Start from capable existing models and test them on local tasks.

2

Retrieval-augmented generation

Ground answers in approved documents that stay under the organization’s control.

3

Fine-tuning

Adapt a model to a domain, language or style where retrieval alone falls short.

4

Specialised models

Build or deeply customise only where validated demand and resources justify it.
